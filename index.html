06 — The ritual

Make them last.

  1. i

    Wrap at night

    A satin bonnet or silk scarf keeps braids smooth and frizz-free.

  2. ii

    Feed the scalp

    A light oil on your parts two or three times a week keeps your scalp happy.

  3. iii

    Refresh, don't soak

    Use a gentle foam or diluted shampoo on the scalp, then dry well.

  4. iv

    Know when to rest

    Take braids out after 6–8 weeks to protect your natural hair.

07 — Good to know

Questions, answered.

How does booking work?

Wiza works by appointment only. Request your preferred date through the form below. Wiza will confirm it with you on WhatsApp, and a 25% upfront payment secures your slot. The balance is paid on the day.

Do you come to clients outside Central Province?

Yes. Wiza is based in Central Province. For appointments outside the province, transport-related costs will reflect in your final bill, and she'll include them in your quote.

Should I come with my hair washed?

Yes, please have clean, detangled, blow-dried hair before your appointment. It makes for a neater, longer-lasting result.

Do I bring my own braiding hair?

Mention your style when you book, and Wiza will tell you exactly what hair to get and how many packs you'll need.

Can I call instead of messaging?

Of course. Call +260 777 091 604 (calls only), or WhatsApp +260 760 877 104.
