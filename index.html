06 — The ritual

Make them last.

  1. i

    Wrap at night

    A satin bonnet or silk scarf keeps braids smooth and frizz-free.

  2. ii

    Feed the scalp

    A light oil on your parts two or three times a week keeps your scalp happy.

  3. iii

    Refresh, don't soak

    Use a gentle foam or diluted shampoo on the scalp, then dry well.

  4. iv

    Know when to rest

    Take braids out after 6–8 weeks to protect your natural hair.

07 — Good to know

Questions, answered.

Should I come with my hair washed?

Yes, please arrive with clean, detangled, blow-dried hair. If you'd rather not, add a wash & blow-dry to your booking.

Is braiding hair included?

You're welcome to bring your own, or Wiza can supply premium pre-stretched hair matched to your colour for a small fee.

How do I secure my booking?

Send your request through the form below. It opens WhatsApp with your details filled in, and Wiza will confirm your date and any deposit there.

Do you do home visits or bridal parties?

Yes. Home visits, bridal parties and group bookings can be arranged. Mention it in your booking note.

Can I bring my daughter?

Of course. Our Little Queens menu is made for young clients, with plenty of patience.
