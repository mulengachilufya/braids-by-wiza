    Aftercare

    1. 1

      Wrap at night

      A satin bonnet or silk scarf keeps braids smooth and stops frizz.

    2. 2

      Oil the scalp

      Use a light oil on your parts two or three times a week.

    3. 3

      Wash gently

      Use a gentle foam or diluted shampoo on the scalp, then dry well.

    4. 4

      Take them out in time

      Remove braids after 6 to 8 weeks to protect your natural hair.

    FAQ

    How does booking work?

    Wiza works by appointment only. Request your date with the form below. Wiza confirms it with you on WhatsApp, and a 25% upfront payment secures your slot. You pay the balance on the day.

    Do you come to me?

    Yes. Wiza is mobile and travels to your location anywhere in Zambia. Transport is added to your price based on where you are, and the price check shows it upfront.

    What's the difference between plain and non plain?

    Plain means you buy and bring your own braiding hair, and you pay for labour only. Non plain means Wiza buys the hair for your style, and its cost is added to your price.

    Should my hair be washed?

    Yes, please have clean, detangled, blow dried hair before your appointment.

    Can I call instead?

    Yes. Call +260 777 091 604 (calls only), or WhatsApp +260 760 877 104.
